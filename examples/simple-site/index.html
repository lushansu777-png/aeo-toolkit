Frequently Asked Questions

What is Acme Analytics?

Acme Analytics is a privacy-first website analytics platform that tracks visitor behavior, heatmaps, and conversions without using cookies. It is GDPR, CCPA, and PECR compliant out of the box.

Is Acme Analytics free?

Yes. Acme Analytics is free forever for 1 website with up to 10,000 monthly pageviews. Pro plans start at $9/month for unlimited sites and advanced features.

Does Acme Analytics use cookies?

No. Acme Analytics uses a cookieless tracking method that identifies unique visitors using anonymized fingerprinting. No consent banner is required.

How is Acme Analytics different from Google Analytics?

Google Analytics uses cookies and requires a consent banner under GDPR. Acme Analytics is cookieless, requires no consent banner, and does not share your visitor data with third parties. Google Analytics is free and has more advanced features; Acme Analytics is simpler and privacy-first.
