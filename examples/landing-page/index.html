Frequently Asked Questions

What is Acme Forms?

Acme Forms is a no-code form builder for SaaS companies that creates onboarding surveys, NPS forms, and feedback widgets optimized for high completion rates. It integrates with popular tools like Segment, Mixpanel, HubSpot, and Slack.

How is Acme Forms different from Typeform or Google Forms?

Acme Forms is purpose-built for SaaS use cases (onboarding, NPS, churn prevention) and achieves higher completion rates through conversational design and smart logic. Typeform is a general-purpose form tool; Google Forms lacks advanced logic and integrations.

Does Acme Forms require coding?

No. Acme Forms is entirely no-code. You build and manage forms in the visual dashboard. A single JavaScript snippet embeds forms on your site in under 2 minutes.

What integrations does Acme Forms support?

Native integrations include Segment, HubSpot, Mixpanel, Intercom, Amplitude, Slack, and Zapier. Full integration list at acme-forms.example.com/integrations.
